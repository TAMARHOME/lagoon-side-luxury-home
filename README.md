# Lagoon Side Luxury Home

Create an ultra-sleek, high-converting luxury real estate and short-let landing page designed for mobile and desktop bookings in Lagos, Nigeria. Use an executive, high-contrast color palette: deep charcoal black (#0F0F0F) backgrounds, subtle slate cards (#1A1A1A), crisp white typography, and warm champagne gold (#D4AF37) accents. Make the layout feel like a million-dollar luxury brand with smooth spacing, subtle borders, and clean typography.

Include these exact 6 sections:

Hero Section: Full-width luxury modern duplex cover image with a rich dark gradient overlay. Top badge: "EXCLUSIVE LISTING • LEKKI PHASE 1". Bold headline: "Luxury 3-Bedroom Waterfront Duplex". Subheadline: "₦150,000 / Night • 24/7 Power • Starlink Wi-Fi • Armed Estate Security". Include a glowing gold primary CTA button labeled "Book Inspection / Reserve on WhatsApp" linking to [https://wa.me/2348000000000?text=Hello,%20I%20am%20interested%20in%20the%20Lekki%20Phase%201%20Duplex](https://wa.me/2348000000000?text=Hello,%20I%20am%20interested%20in%20the%20Lekki%20Phase%201%20Duplex).

Video Tour & Advert Showcase: A dedicated "Property Walkthrough & Video Tour" section right below the hero. Feature a sleek, widescreen/vertical-friendly custom video player card with a gold play button overlay, duration badge ("01:45 HD Tour"), and a clean caption ("Take a 2-minute virtual walkthrough of the interior, compound, and neighborhood"). Include a toggle or clean placeholder embed so the owner can easily swap in a 1–2 minute MP4 video file, YouTube link, or Instagram/TikTok advert reel.

Property Photo Gallery: A responsive, editorial-style 4-image grid showcasing a modern living room, master bedroom, fitted kitchen, and swimming pool with full-screen lightbox preview on click.

Key Amenities & Specs: Clean dark-slate cards with gold icons for: 3 En-Suite Bedrooms, 24/7 Uninterrupted Power, High-Speed Starlink Internet, 24/7 Armed Estate Security, Swimming Pool & Gym, and Dedicated Parking.

Host / Agent Trust Section: An executive profile card featuring the agent's photo placeholder, agency name ("PrimeHaven Properties Lagos"), gold verified badge, short bio ("Trusted luxury short-let and property specialists in Lekki & Victoria Island"), and 2 concise 5-star client testimonials.

Sticky Bottom Mobile Action Bar & Footer: A sleek, frosted-glass fixed bottom bar on mobile screens displaying "₦150,000 / night" on the left and a high-contrast WhatsApp button ("Chat on WhatsApp") on the right so potential clients can book immediately while watching the video or scrolling.
